ABOUT US

Punlaan is a web-based e-commerce marketplace designed for the buying and selling of plants in the Philippines. The platform enables multiple plant sellers to create and manage their own online stores while allowing customers to conveniently browse, compare, and purchase a wide variety of seedlings, bushes, and saplings from a single website. By bringing buyers and sellers together in one centralized marketplace, Punlaan provides an organized, accessible, and user-friendly shopping experience that simplifies plant discovery, promotes transparent pricing, and supports the growth of local plant businesses.

MISSION

Punlaan's overall goal is to help make buying and selling plants and saplings a much more convenient and accessible marketplace by giving these people a website to give them a much easier access to sell and buy.

VISION

To become the leading online marketplace for plants in the Philippines, empowering local growers and connecting communities through sustainable agriculture and accessible green commerce.
