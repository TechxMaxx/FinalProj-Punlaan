Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

The short version
  • We only collect what we need to run the marketplace: your account, your orders, and your delivery address.
  • Sellers only see the delivery details of orders placed with them.
  • We never sell your personal information.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

Punlaan ("we", "us") is an online marketplace where people in the Philippines buy and sell plants, seedlings, saplings and garden supplies. This policy explains how we handle personal information, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: first and last name, email address, password (stored only in scrambled, hashed form — we can't read it), and optionally a mobile number, a short "About me", and a profile photo.
  • Delivery addresses: recipient name, mobile number, region, province, city or municipality, barangay, street and postal code. If you choose to, the exact map location (latitude and longitude) of the address.
  • Orders: what you bought, quantities, prices, the delivery address used, and the order's status.
  • Saved plants and cart items.
  • If you apply to sell: shop name, shop description, mobile number, a photo or scan of a valid government ID, and optionally a business permit.
  • If you sell: your product listings and photos.

Information from sign-in providers

If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address and the provider's account ID for you. We use this only to create or log in to your Punlaan account. We don't receive your Google or Facebook password and we don't post anything on your behalf.

Information collected automatically

  • A session cookie that keeps you logged in while you use the site.
  • Your location, only if you press "Use my current location" when adding an address — your browser asks for your permission first. It's used to place the map pin and fill in the address.
  • Standard server logs kept by our web host (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and troubleshooting.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and to let you sign in (including with Google or Facebook).
  • To process orders: show sellers what to pack and where to deliver, and show you your order status.
  • To review seller applications and product listings before they appear in the marketplace.
  • To send account emails you ask for, such as password reset links.
  • To keep the marketplace safe: preventing fraud, and suspending or blocking accounts that break our Terms of Service.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for third-party advertising.

4. Who can see your information

WhoWhat they can see
Sellers you order from Your name, the delivery address and mobile number on that order (and its map pin, if you added one), and what you ordered. Only for orders placed with them.
Other shoppers For sellers only: the shop name, shop description, listings and number of items sold. Shoppers' personal details are never shown to other shoppers.
Punlaan administrators Account details, orders, seller applications and submitted ID documents, to run and moderate the marketplace. ID documents are stored privately and can only be opened by a logged-in administrator.
Authorities Only when required by law or a valid legal order.

5. Outside services we use

  • Google Sign-In and Facebook Login — only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • PSGC API (Philippine Standard Geographic Code, Philippine Statistics Authority data) — to list regions, provinces, cities and barangays. It doesn't receive your personal information.
  • OpenStreetMap (map display and Nominatim address lookup) — when you use the map pin, the pinned coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap to find the matching address.
  • Gmail — used to send password reset emails to you.
  • Our web hosting provider — stores the website and database.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

6. Cookies and browser storage

We use one essential session cookie to keep you logged in. We also temporarily store the list of Philippine places in your browser so the address form loads faster. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. How we protect your information

  • Passwords are hashed; password reset links expire after 30 minutes and work only once.
  • Seller ID documents are kept in a private folder that can't be opened from the web.
  • Administrator tools require an administrator login.
  • Uploaded files are checked to make sure they're real images or PDFs.

No website is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we'll notify you and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. Order records may be kept longer where needed for accounting, disputes or legal requirements. Seller ID documents from an earlier application are replaced when you apply again, and are deleted when your account is deleted.

9. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is used (this policy).
  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct it — most details can be edited yourself in Profile.
  • Object to processing, or withdraw consent.
  • Have it deleted or blocked.
  • Data portability — get a copy in a common format.
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

10. Deleting your account and data

To delete your Punlaan account and personal information:

  1. Email us at [support email] from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account".
  2. We'll confirm the request and delete your account, addresses, saved plants, cart and uploaded photos and documents within 30 days.
  3. Records of past orders may be kept without your name where the law requires it.

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove Punlaan from your Facebook settings (Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites) and then email us as above.

11. Children

Punlaan is not meant for children under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We'll change the date at the top and, for important changes, let you know on the website.

13. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Punlaan team at [support email].
