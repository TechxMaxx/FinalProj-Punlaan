The short version
  • Punlaan connects plant buyers with independent local sellers.
  • Sellers are verified by our team, and every listing is reviewed before it goes live.
  • Pay the seller on delivery; you can cancel until the seller packs your order.
  • Only sell legal plants and garden products, and describe them honestly.

1. About Punlaan

Punlaan is an online marketplace in the Philippines for plants, seedlings, saplings and garden supplies. Products are listed and sold by independent sellers ("Sellers"), not by Punlaan. By creating an account or using the website you agree to these Terms and to our Privacy Policy.

2. Your account

  • You must be at least 18 years old to create an account.
  • Give accurate information and keep it up to date.
  • Keep your password private. You're responsible for activity on your account.
  • You may sign in with email and password, Google, or Facebook. These all lead to the same account when they use the same email address.
  • One person may not use multiple accounts to get around a suspension or block.

3. Buying

  • Prices and stock are set by Sellers. The price you pay is the price shown at checkout.
  • Shipping is a flat fee shown at checkout (currently ₱150 per order).
  • Payment: orders are paid on delivery, directly for the items you receive. Punlaan does not currently take online payments.
  • After you place an order, the Seller prepares it and updates its status: To Pack → Packed → Shipping → Shipped → Completed. You can follow this in My Orders.
  • Plants are living things. Their size, color and shape may differ slightly from the photos.
  • When you receive your order, press Order received so the order is marked completed.

4. Cancelling orders

  • You can cancel an item while it's still To Pack — before the Seller packs it.
  • Sellers can cancel an item before it's handed to the courier (for example, if a plant is no longer healthy). They should tell you why.
  • Items that are shipping, shipped or completed can't be cancelled through the website. Contact the Seller or Punlaan for help.

5. Selling on Punlaan

  • Applying: you apply from your Profile with your shop details and a valid government ID. Our team reviews every application; we may approve or decline it. You can keep shopping while you wait.
  • Listings: every new or edited listing is reviewed by our team before it appears in the marketplace. Listings need at least one real photo of the product.
  • Honesty: describe plants accurately — species, size, condition — and use your own photos.
  • Fulfilling orders: pack plants carefully, update the order status promptly, and ship only to the delivery address on the order.
  • Buyer information: use buyers' names, addresses and phone numbers only to deliver their orders. Don't contact them for other purposes or share their details.
  • Laws: you're responsible for following laws that apply to your business, including plant quarantine rules and any permits or registrations you need.

6. Items not allowed

You may not list or sell:

  • Wild-collected, threatened or protected plants, or anything whose trade is restricted under the Wildlife Resources Conservation and Protection Act (Republic Act No. 9147) or similar laws, without the required permits.
  • Illegal or controlled plants and substances.
  • Invasive species or plants and soil that break plant quarantine regulations.
  • Pesticides or chemicals that are banned or not registered for sale in the Philippines.
  • Counterfeit, stolen or misrepresented items, or anything that isn't a plant, gardening product or garden tool.
